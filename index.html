Professional Background

My career combines entrepreneurship, hotel operations, and hospitality technology, giving me experience across the commercial and operational sides of the industry.

I began in my family's footwear manufacturing business, where I managed production and distribution alongside a 25-person team. Working in a live SME environment taught me the fundamentals of business operations — from quality control and brand management to market research and execution — building a practical understanding of how commercial decisions translate into operational results.

After relocating to Thailand, I transitioned into hospitality, developing a comprehensive understanding of hotel operations through progressively broader responsibilities. As a Guest Service Agent, I gained frontline experience in guest relations, service recovery, and cross-department coordination. Moving into Financial Income Auditor, I strengthened my commercial knowledge by working with rate structures, ADR, RevPAR, OTA performance, and condominium leasing contracts. Later, as Guest Service Supervisor, I led operational execution across multiple departments, coordinating a workforce of 70+ staff during shifts while managing escalated guest issues and operational incidents in real time.

Current Role

I currently work as a Sales & Revenue Executive in hospitality technology, representing RevBoost's revenue management solutions and services.

My role focuses on commercial growth through consultative selling. Rather than leading with a product pitch, I begin by diagnosing operational and revenue challenges — such as OTA rate inconsistencies, booking engine conversion gaps, and distribution inefficiencies — and translate those findings into measurable commercial opportunities for prospective clients.

This approach combines business analysis with relationship building, enabling me to establish credibility with hotel owners and decision-makers while positioning technology as a solution to identifiable revenue challenges. Alongside sales execution, I continue expanding my expertise in hospitality operations, revenue optimization, and AI-driven technologies shaping the industry's future.

Looking Ahead

Over the next five years, I aim to build a reputation as a commercial leader who combines strong relationship-building, clear communication, and technical expertise to deliver measurable business outcomes in the hospitality industry.
